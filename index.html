Realms I

Coming Soon Release Date TBD RCU Project

Official Synopsis

After years of training in the wake of their father's death, brothers Ben and Max discover an ancient gateway capable of opening passages to worlds beyond their own. Driven by unanswered questions about their father's past, the two cross into a dangerous realm filled with strange creatures, forgotten ruins, and powers neither of them fully understands.

But as the realm begins awakening something within them, the brothers find themselves divided over what that power should be used for. What begins as a journey for answers soon becomes a fight over family, grief, and the temptation of becoming powerful enough to control what was once feared.

With forces beyond their understanding closing in, Ben and Max must decide how far they're willing to go—and whether their bond can survive what waits beyond the gate.

Announced Release Date TBD Before Division Zero

Official Synopsis

Operating alone in the neon-lit underworld of a sprawling cyberpunk city, Jack Walker makes his living completing dangerous missions without asking questions. But during what should have been another routine operation, Jack uncovers evidence that his employer has been experimenting with unstable realm energy—and innocent people are paying the price.

Refusing to remain part of the operation, Jack turns against the people who hired him and becomes their next target. Hunted across rooftops, alleyways, and heavily guarded facilities, he must use everything he's learned to uncover the full extent of the conspiracy before the technology threatens more than just one city.

With nowhere left to hide and enemies closing in, Jack must choose what kind of man he intends to become when following orders is no longer an option.

Official Synopsis

Years after the events that changed their lives forever, Ben and Max find themselves drawn back into a conflict spanning the Realms.

When a mysterious masked attacker begins hunting Ben through a vast neon city, signs emerge that the threat may be connected to forces the brothers once believed they had left behind. Old wounds resurface as the search for answers pulls them deeper into a world of corrupted energy, hidden enemies, and dangers capable of reaching across the boundaries between realms.

With an unknown enemy growing stronger and the past refusing to stay buried, Ben and Max must confront what remains between them and determine whether they can stand together against a threat neither brother can face alone.
